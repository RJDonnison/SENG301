# Ethics Bodies

Professional bodies establish codes of ethics to guide software engineers in making responsible decisions. See [General Ethics](/ethics/general.md).

## ACM (Association for Computing Machinery)

The ACM is the world's largest scientific and educational computing society. Its **ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct** outlines the responsibilities of computing professionals, organized into four core principles:

1. **Public Interest:** Contribute to society and human well-being, placing the public good above personal or organizational interests.
2. **Professional Responsibilities:** Strive to achieve high quality in professional work, maintain integrity, and uphold the reputation of the profession.
3. **Professional Leadership:** Promote ethical conduct within organizations, foster public understanding of computing, and encourage responsible participation.
4. **Compliance with the Code:** Uphold and support the code, report violations when appropriate, and avoid retaliation against those who raise ethical concerns.

The ACM also provides detailed case studies and guidelines to help practitioners apply these principles in real-world scenarios.

## IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)

The IEEE is a leading professional organization for electrical, electronics, and computing engineers. Its **IEEE Code of Ethics** commits members to:

1. Hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public.
2. Avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest and disclose them when they arise.
3. Be honest and realistic in stating claims or estimates based on available data.
4. Reject bribery and corruption in all forms.
5. Improve the understanding of technology and its appropriate application.
6. Maintain and improve technical competence and undertake technological tasks only if qualified.
7. Treat all persons fairly and respectfully, and avoid discrimination.
8. Accept and provide honest criticism of technical work, acknowledge and correct errors.
9. Assist colleagues and coworkers in their professional development.
