# STAR Framework

Used for **behavioural interview answers** and **retrospective storytelling**.

| Component     | Meaning             | SWE Example                                                                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **S**ituation | Set the context     | "Our CI pipeline was taking 40 mins per build..."                                              |
| **T**ask      | Your responsibility | "I was tasked with reducing build time to under 10 mins"                                       |
| **A**ction    | What _you_ did      | "I parallelised test suites, added caching for dependencies, and removed redundant lint steps" |
| **R**esult    | Measurable outcome  | "Build time dropped to 8 mins, unblocking 6 engineers daily"                                   |

## When to use

- Behavioural interview questions ("Tell me about a time you...")
- Performance review self-assessments
- Post-mortems and incident retrospectives

## Tips for SWEs

- Make the **Result quantifiable** — latency %, uptime, tickets closed, velocity gained
- Keep Situation and Task brief (20%), spend most time on Action and Result (80%)
- Avoid "we did" — own your individual contribution clearly
