# Agile

Agile is an iterative and incremental approach to software development and project management that emphasizes collaboration, flexibility, continuous improvement, and high-quality results. It prioritizes adapting to change over strictly following a plan, delivering working software frequently, and fostering self-organizing, cross-functional teams. The core principles of Agile are articulated in the Agile Manifesto.

## Key Principles of Agile

*   **Individuals and Interactions over Processes and Tools:** Agile values direct communication and collaboration among team members and stakeholders more than rigid processes or sophisticated tools.
*   **Working Software over Comprehensive Documentation:** The primary measure of progress in Agile is functional software that delivers value to the customer, rather than extensive documentation.
*   **Customer Collaboration over Contract Negotiation:** Agile emphasizes continuous interaction with customers throughout the development lifecycle to ensure the product meets their evolving needs.
*   **Responding to Change over Following a Plan:** Agile methodologies are designed to be adaptable, allowing teams to respond quickly to changes in requirements, market conditions, or feedback.

## Benefits of Agile

*   **Faster Delivery:** Incremental development allows for quicker delivery of valuable features.
*   **Increased Flexibility:** The ability to adapt to changing requirements reduces risk and improves product relevance.
*   **Improved Quality:** Continuous testing and feedback loops lead to higher quality software.
*   **Enhanced Collaboration:** Agile fosters better communication and teamwork among all involved parties.
*   **Greater Customer Satisfaction:** Regular delivery of working software and active customer involvement ensures the product aligns with user needs.
